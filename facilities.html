Clinical Infrastructure

World-Class Facilities & Medical Technology

Equipped with state-of-the-art diagnostic, surgical and critical care infrastructure in Guntur.

100-Bed Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

Dedicated Medical ICU, Surgical ICU, Neuro ICU, Cardiac ICU & Level-III NICU. Features HEPA airflow filters, central monitoring consoles & dedicated 1:1 patient nursing ratio.

Flat-Panel Digital Cath Lab

Advanced cardiac catheterization lab system performing 24/7 primary angioplasty, stenting, pacemaker implantation and electrophysiology studies with minimum X-ray radiation dose.

8 Modular Operation Theatres

Ultra-clean surgical suites equipped with positive pressure laminar airflow, seamless anti-bacterial paneling, modern anesthesia workstations & C-Arm fluoroscopy.

3T MRI & 128-Slice CT Imaging

Ultra-high resolution magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) and multi-detector 128-slice CT scanner for rapid cardiac, neuro, vascular, chest and abdominal cross-sectional imaging.

Video Endoscopy & Bronchoscopy Suite

High-definition video endoscopes for diagnostic and therapeutic upper GI endoscopy, colonoscopy, ERCP stone retrieval & pulmonology bronchoscopy.

24/7 Emergency, Pharmacy & Lab

Fully integrated 24/7 emergency triage center, round-the-clock inpatient pharmacy, blood component bank access & automated central pathology laboratory.
