Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Aditya Medicare Hospitals, Guntur ("we", "our", "us") is committed to safeguarding patient privacy and protecting personal medical information collected through our website and hospital reception desks.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal details including patient name, phone number, email address, appointment preferences, and medical inquiry notes submitted via website booking forms or phone consultations.

2. Use of Information

Information collected is strictly utilized to facilitate doctor appointment scheduling, emergency medical response, hospital administrative communications, patient care coordination, and healthcare service improvements.

3. Data Security & Confidentiality

We enforce strict technical and organizational safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of patient information in compliance with applicable healthcare privacy regulations.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade patient personal data to third parties. Data is shared only with authorized medical professionals involved in patient treatment or when required by legal mandate.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy or wish to update your records, please contact our hospital administrative desk at info@adityamedicarehospitals.com or call 0863-294-4444.
