Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Aditya Medicare Hospitals uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored on your browser or device when you visit a website. They allow websites to remember user preferences, maintain session security, and optimize performance.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

We use essential cookies strictly necessary for core website navigation, appointment modal functionality, and security. We may also use anonymous performance analytics cookies to improve site loading speed and user experience.

3. Managing Cookies

You can choose to disable or clear cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may impact certain interactive features such as online appointment booking.
